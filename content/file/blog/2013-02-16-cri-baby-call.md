# Cri "baby" Call

[![Cri_Call_Tube_baby_monitor_front](assets/old_assets/643_cri_call_tube_baby_monitor_front.jpg)](assets/old_assets/Cri_Call_Tube_baby_monitor_front.jpg)
I found this at work, at first it looked like some kind of intercom but then I realised it is called a "Cri Call". It is an old valve based baby monitor! What you do is screw the small box onto the wall in the babys room then trail a wire along your hallway to your living room to monitor the baby. Next test, put a guitar into it! it will definitely make a neat (tube!) practice amp.
