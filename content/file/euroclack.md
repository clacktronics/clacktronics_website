# EuroClack

EuroClack modules are made by me — some are kits, others are information for free.

## Modules

1. **[Mini-Speaker kit](window:file/euroclack/mini-speaker.md)** — a compact audio monitor for portable or monitor-free patches.
2. **[Proto-PSU kit](window:file/euroclack/proto-psu.md)** — a beginner-friendly dual-rail supply for breadboards and small systems.
3. **[Analogue Voltmeter kit](window:file/euroclack/analogue-meter.md)** — vintage needle display with three buffered CV outputs.
