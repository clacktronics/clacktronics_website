Web Serial needs Chrome, Edge or Opera over HTTPS. The port is chosen by you, per session.

Demo device

A simulated device on the other end of the console, for trying the decoders and the sender out without hardware — and for browsers with no Web Serial at all.

MIDIterm bridge

DIN MIDI on a UART is still MIDI once something puts it on a port, and MIDIterm is that something. Open it in another window and the bytes off this port play out of a real MIDI output — and what MIDIterm hears comes back down the wire. It never leaves the browser.

Macros

Each macro sends in its own mode. Hex accepts 0x41 42 and "text", and the send line ending is added to text macros.
