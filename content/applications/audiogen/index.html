audioGen is a local-first text-to-audio workspace for ClackOS. The interface reads from a shared model catalog, while each model family is implemented by a worker adapter, leaving room for future music and sound-effect generators. Prompts and generated audio stay on this device; model weights are downloaded from the Hugging Face Hub and cached by your browser.
