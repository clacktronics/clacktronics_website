PCB Heater Designer

PCB heater geometry, electrical checks and KiCad board export. Dimensions are finished-copper design values.

Used by fractal curves only.

Pattern notes
Advantage: Simple, predictable single-layer routing with accessible edge pads, straightforward length control and easy fabrication.
Trade-off: Parallel runs can produce visible hot and cool bands, especially with wide pitch or poor heat spreading.
Input definitions
Power
Target power at the selected copper temperature.
Copper
Finished copper weight after fabrication.
Pitch
Centre-to-centre spacing of adjacent serpentine or spiral tracks.
Iteration
Recursive depth; each pattern receives a safe default when selected.
Radius
Requested trace corner radius, reduced locally where needed.
Drill
Used only for through-hole connection pads.

Exports black board with white copper, pads and a thin board outline; drill holes remain black. Export front and back separately for two-layer designs.

Electrical assumptions and safety
  • Power is calculated for constant-voltage DC at the entered copper temperature.
  • Copper temperature coefficient is 0.00393/°C; cold inrush is therefore higher.
  • 1 oz is modelled as 0.0348 mm finished copper. Etch width and plating tolerances are not included.
  • Via, pad and connector resistance are omitted from the width calculation.
  • 5 W/cm² is only a conservative warning threshold, not a guaranteed FR-4 thermal limit.
  • Prototype with current limiting, a fuse and an independent temperature cutoff. Verify resistance before full power.
