Modded Realistic Reverb 2

5 September 2011


I made this for Owen at Flesh and Bone Studios in Hackney after he saw this original post. I had a bit more time to make it properly and I tried to make it look a bit more like the original case - it strangely ended up looking a bit more like a Roger Mayer pedal. Pretty much the same mods so see the link to the original post.

The enclosure came out quite nice - the process was to apply lazertran and fuse it on in the oven for 2 hours then I sprayed a few light layers of Acrylic Laquer which created a really nice stippled effect.

I also made another video for it but this time with a Wasp.
