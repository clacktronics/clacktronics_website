About ClackOS

Clacktronics

Hardware for people who fail to read the datasheet twice.

Welcome to CLACKTRONICS a project and company site for all things made by Ben Barwise. Based in a shed of North-East London in Epping forest I build and design electronic equipment and solutions for Artists and musicians. I have 20+ years experience as a technologist in music and art and am a PCB design specialist.

What is clackOS?

A corny mock operating system that has a number of useful apps and content for music, art and just about anything else I can think of. Most apps are either derived by me or open source projects, you will find credits at the bottom. Every thing is client side and managed using an open Github repository. If you see any issues please let me know in the bug report found in the File menu.
