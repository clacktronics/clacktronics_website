About ClackOS

Clacktronics

Hardware for people who dont bother to read the datasheet twice.

Welcome to CLACKTRONICS a project and company site for all things made by Ben Barwise. Based in a shed in North-East London, Epping forest, I build and design electronic equipment and solutions for artists and musicians. I have 20+ years experience as a technologist in music and art and am a PCB design specialist. I am currently a full-time technician at UCL in East London.

What is clackOS?

A corny mock operating system that has a number of useful apps and content for music, art and just about anything else I can think of. Most apps are either derived by me or open source projects, you will find credits at the bottom of each app. The whole site runs client side and managed using an open Github repository. If you see any issues please let me know in the bug report found in the File menu. It will work ok on mobile, but its best viewed on desktop.
